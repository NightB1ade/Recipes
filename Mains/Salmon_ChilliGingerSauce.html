--- title: Salmon with Chilli Ginger Sauce basePortions: 4 ---

Ingredients

  • balls of stem ginger in syrup
  • tbs of the syrup
  • garlic cloves
  • tbs dark soya sauce
  • well-scrubbed orange, finely grated zest
  • freshly squeezed orange juice (about ml)
  • long red chilli, thinly sliced
  • × 125–150g salmon fillets, skin on
  • Freshly ground black pepper

Method

  1. Put the ginger balls to make thin matchstick strips
  2. Add the stem ginger syrup from the jar
  3. Peel the garlic cloves and slice them thinly, then add them to the bowl with the ginger
  4. Stir in the soya sauce, orange zest, orange juice, and red chilli
  5. Put the salmon in the bowl with the marinade
  6. Season with lots of ground black pepper and turn a couple times, ending with the fish skin side up
  7. Cover and chill for 30 minutes
  8. Preheat the oven to 220°C/Fan 200°C/Gas 7
  9. Line a small baking tray with baking parchment
  10. Take the salmon fillets out of the marinade, scraping off any bits and pieces, and place them on the tray, skin side down
  11. Season with more ground black pepper
  12. Bake for 12–15 minutes
  13. Pour the marinade into a small saucepan and bring to the boil
  14. Cook for 6 monutes or until the liquid has reduced and the garlic is softened
